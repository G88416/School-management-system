📋 Testing Instructions

  1. Open this page on two different devices (or use two browsers/incognito windows)
  2. Enter your name on each device
  3. Enter the same room name on both devices (default: "test-room")
  4. Click "Join Room" on both devices
  5. Allow camera and microphone permissions when prompted
  6. Verify: You should see both video feeds
  7. Test: Try muting/unmuting and toggling video

✅ Success Criteria: Both users can see and hear each other with minimal delay (< 1 second)
